# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue about the work, not the person making it.

## What is expected

- Give and take criticism about code, design and decisions directly and without hedging. Blunt
  technical disagreement is welcome and is not a conduct problem.
- Assume the other person has context you lack before concluding they are wrong.
- Accept that "no" is a legitimate answer to a proposal, and that a maintainer saying it owes you
  a reason but not a debate.

## What is not

- Personal attacks, insults, or making someone's identity the subject.
- Harassment of any kind, in the repo or anywhere else the project is discussed.
- Sustained disruption — reopening settled decisions without new information, or demanding
  unpaid work from maintainers.
- Publishing anyone's private information.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere someone is representing the
project.

## Reporting

Email the maintainer at deenismp@gmail.com. Reports are read by one person and kept private.

There is no committee here — this is a small project with one maintainer, so enforcement means a
warning, then removal from the repo's interactions. That is the whole range of available
responses, and pretending otherwise would be theatre.
